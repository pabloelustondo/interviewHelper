# Slide 1 — Purpose

**Visual:** [slide.svg](./slide.svg)

- Meeting Helper is a local browser prototype that tells a speaker, at a glance, how long they have been talking.
- Interview use case: answers should be concise. You should not have to read anything, only see a color.
- Green "GOOD" for 0–45 s, yellow "LAND IT" for 45–75 s, red "STOP" after 75 s.
- Everything runs locally in the browser: plain HTML and JavaScript, no frameworks, no backend, no AI yet.
