# Slide 7 — Limits and roadmap

**Visual:** [slide.puml](./slide.puml)

- Working today: hear, detect, and time (green).
- Known limitation: any sufficiently loud sound advances the timer, including the interviewer or background noise.
- Next steps from [Plan.md](../../Plan.md): validate turn boundaries, calibrate thresholds with real speaking, then identify the speaker, transcribe, and finally add semantic coaching.
- Principle: keep the deterministic layers underneath. Do not jump from microphone straight to an AI agent.
